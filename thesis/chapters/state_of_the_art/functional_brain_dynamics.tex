\section{Representation Learning of Functional Brain Dynamics}

reference papers:
\begin{itemize}
    \item “Generative Embeddings of Brain Collective Dynamics Using Variational Autoencoders” — Yonatan Sanz Perl et al. (2020). VAE applied to brain activity, low-dimensional embedding of collective brain dynamics, analysis of meaningful organization within the learned latent space.
    \item “Representation Learning of Resting State fMRI with Variational Autoencoder” — Jung-Hoon Kim, Yizhen Zhang, Kuan Han, Zheyu Wen, Minkyu Choi \& Zhongming Liu (2021). Convolutional VAE applied directly to resting-state fMRI, individual fMRI frames represented in a low-dimensional latent space, analysis of brain activity through learned representations.
    \item “Low-Dimensional Organization of Global Brain States of Reduced Consciousness” — Yonatan Sanz Perl et al. (2023). VAE applied to parcellated resting-state fMRI, low-dimensional representation of global brain states, comparison of latent organization between different states of consciousness.
    \item “Modelling Low-Dimensional Interacting Brain Networks Reveals Organising Principle in Human Cognition” — Yonatan Sanz Perl et al. (2025). Low-dimensional modelling of fMRI brain activity, interpretation of latent variables through functional brain networks, analysis of interactions and coupling between networks in the latent representation.
\end{itemize}
