reference:
\begin{itemize}
    \item “Krakencoder: A Unified Brain Connectome Translation and Fusion Tool” — Keith W. Jamison, Zijin Gu, Qinxin Wang, Ceren Tozlu, Mert R. Sabuncu \& Amy Kuceyeski (2025). Low-dimensional representation of functional brain networks, latent-space interpretation of network interactions, relevant to analysing how latent dimensions relate to functional brain organization.
